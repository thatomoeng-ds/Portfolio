Volunteering Deep Dive

Mhlanga Rikhotso Foundation

Career Services Officer

The Mhlanga Rikhotso Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering high school learners from underserved communities through education, mentorship, career guidance, and personal development opportunities. As a Career Services Officer, I support learners in exploring suitable academic and professional pathways and building the confidence to pursue their goals.

Key Contributions

  • Provided career guidance and mentorship to high school learners, helping them explore suitable academic and professional pathways.
  • Assisted learners in understanding tertiary education opportunities, admission requirements, bursaries, and career options.
  • Facilitated discussions and engagement sessions aimed at building confidence, goal-setting, and decision-making skills.
  • Supported educational initiatives designed to improve access to information and opportunities for learners from disadvantaged communities.
  • Contributed to the Foundation's mission of using education as a catalyst for long-term social and economic upliftment.

Skills Developed

  • Mentorship & Coaching
  • Communication & Presentation
  • Stakeholder Engagement
  • Leadership
  • Public Speaking
  • Community Development

Impact

Through my role as a Career Services Officer, I have supported learners in making informed decisions about their education and future careers, helping to create opportunities for personal growth and long-term success.
