BMW Social Squad

The BMW Social Squad is an employee-driven initiative focused on fostering a positive workplace culture through creative engagement activities, brand activations, and team-building experiences. As an Ideas & Innovation member, I collaborate with colleagues to develop and execute initiatives that strengthen employee engagement and promote a vibrant workplace environment.

Key Contributions

  • Contributed creative ideas and planning support for employee engagement and workplace culture initiatives.
  • Participated in the FIFA World Cup department decoration project, helping create an engaging and collaborative work environment.
  • Supported the International Bikers Day activation for BMW Motorrad, celebrating the motorcycle community and enhancing employee participation.
  • Assisted with the Spider-Man x BMW iX3 launch activation, contributing to internal engagement and brand awareness activities.
  • Collaborated with cross-functional teams to organise and execute employee-focused events and campaigns.
  • Helped strengthen team morale, workplace connectivity, and employee engagement through innovative social initiatives.

Skills Developed

  • Event Planning & Coordination
  • Creative Problem Solving
  • Team Collaboration
  • Stakeholder Engagement
  • Communication
  • Project Coordination
  • Innovation & Ideation

Impact

Through the BMW Social Squad, I have contributed to initiatives that enhance employee engagement, strengthen workplace culture, and create memorable experiences that connect people to the BMW brand beyond their day-to-day roles.
